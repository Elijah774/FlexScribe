FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

What is FlexScribe?

FlexScribe is a personal finance dashboard that helps you track subscriptions, manage a virtual wallet, set savings goals, and understand your spending — all in one place.

What plans are available?

We offer three plans: Individual (R149/mo) for personal use, Professional (R299/mo) with full analytics and AI insights, and Family (R499/mo) which lets you manage dependants and their finances from one account.

How secure is my financial data?

We use AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. We never sell your data. You can enable two-factor authentication for added protection.

Do I need to link my bank account?

No. FlexScribe works as a standalone tracker. You manually add subscriptions and fund your virtual wallet. Bank linking is an optional future feature.

What is a Scribe Score?

Your Scribe Score (0–850) measures your financial health based on subscription regularity, budget adherence and savings rate. Higher scores unlock rewards and badges.

Can I manage subscriptions for my family?

Yes — with the Family plan (R499/mo) you can add dependants, generate their own login credentials, and manage their subscriptions. Dependants can request subscriptions which you approve and pay for, or they can add their own independently.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Absolutely. There are no long-term contracts. Cancel at any time and you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period.

Is FlexScribe available on mobile?

The web app is fully responsive and works great on mobile browsers. Native iOS and Android apps are on our roadmap for later this year.
